Overview

What is CineList?

CineList is a movie discovery and management platform that connects cinephiles with the films they love. Browse trending and upcoming releases, dive deep into cast details and reviews, and keep your personal Watchlist and Wishlist in one beautifully curated space. Every feature is designed to make managing your film life effortless and enjoyable.

Features

What you can do

Search Films

Find any movie by title instantly across TMDB's vast catalogue.

Rich Movie Pages

Posters, genres, ratings, overviews, and full cast information at a glance.

Watchlist

Save films to watch later and track everything you plan to see.

Wishlist

Curate a personal list of films you're most eager to experience.

Reviews

Read and write reviews. Let the community know what's worth watching.

Browse Users

Discover what other cinephiles are watching and reviewing.
